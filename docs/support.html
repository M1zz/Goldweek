🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

Goldweek does not collect personal information. All data you enter — including your name, leave settings, and leave records — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

We use Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchase processing. Purchase-related data is handled according to Apple's Privacy Policy. No third-party analytics tools or advertising SDKs are used.

Data Security

All data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud Backup is enabled, your data is protected by Apple's encryption policies.
