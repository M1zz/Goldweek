Turn holidays into long breaks

Goldweek finds the days off that stretch your PTO the furthest.
It pairs public holidays with weekends and shows you how to get the longest break for the fewest PTO days.

What Goldweek does

✨

Smart suggestions

For every upcoming holiday, see how many days to take off and how many days in a row you will get. You will never miss a bridge day again.

🌏

Holidays in 42 countries

Public holidays for the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and 36 more countries across Europe, Asia, Latin America, the Middle East and Africa. Where holidays vary by state or region, just pick yours. Country not listed? Add your own holidays. You can also choose your weekend days and the first day of the week.

📅

PTO tracking

See your total, used and remaining days on one screen. Log half days and quarter days, and keep track of extra leave such as comp days along with their expiration dates. Tap a date in the calendar to add or edit time off, or import past leave from a photo of your HR screen.

📱

Widget and backup

Your remaining PTO and upcoming time off sit right on your Home Screen. Your records are backed up to iCloud, and you can share your schedule with family. Works on iPhone and iPad.
